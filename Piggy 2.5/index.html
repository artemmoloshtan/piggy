Frequently Asked Questions

Which tax jurisdiction is Piggy built for?

Piggy is specifically designed for the Czech Republic. It enforces the rules of the Czech Income Tax Act (Zákon o daních z příjmů č. 586/1992 Sb.): the 3-year holding period exemption ("časový test"), the 100,000 CZK threshold under § 4(1)(w), currency translation using GFŘ uniform exchange rates, and the FIFO pairing method aligned with the 2019 Supreme Administrative Court (NSS) ruling.

Why does the input format use Czech broker headers?

The standard columns match the export format from Czech brokerages (specifically FIO e-Broker): Datum obchodu;Směr;Symbol;Cena;Počet;Měna;Objem;Poplatky. Keeping standard naming allows you to load trade history directly from your broker export with zero manual reformatting.

Can this report be submitted as tax evidence?

Yes. The exported piggy-report.csv or the Print/PDF output provides the exact trade-by-trade pairings and calculations that financial offices (finanční úřad) expect to see as supporting documentation for § 10 of your tax return.
